# Chez Toi BH app privacy policy

Last updated: [publication date]

This policy explains what data the **Chez Toi BH** app ("the app") uses, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights. It follows Brazil’s data protection law (Lei 13.709/2018, "LGPD").

The app is only for students who live in Chez Toi BH apartments in Belo Horizonte: only people with a stay at Chez Toi can sign in.

## 2. Data the app uses

### 2.1 Account and stay (from your Chez Toi record)
- **E-mail and name** on your contract, to sign you in and identify you.
- **Password**: we only keep an encrypted version (hash); no one on the team can read it.
- **Stay details** already in your record: apartment, address, move-in and move-out dates, your roommates’ first names, contract, rent and payment status, deposit, cleanings and the house rules you accepted.

### 2.2 First-access check
- **Date of birth and passport number**: on first access you type them and we compare them with your record to confirm it is you. The app **does not keep a copy** of what you typed; only the attempt is logged (with the e-mail turned into an irreversible code) to block people trying to guess.

### 2.3 What you send through the app
- **Requests** (repairs, extra services, stay changes) and **the photos** you attach: the Chez Toi team sees them to handle the request.
- **Reviews, surveys and referrals**.
- **Visitors** you announce to the front desk (name and date) and **packages** you expect.
- **House chores and shared expenses** with your roommates.
- **Community**: posts, comments, group trips, event attendance, reports and blocks, and the optional profile (school, hometown, languages, Instagram, about), visible to other residents as you choose.

### 2.4 Charlotte, the assistant (artificial intelligence)
- When you ask Charlotte a question, **the question and any photo you attach** are sent, through our server, to **Claude, Anthropic’s artificial intelligence**, together with the excerpts needed to answer (your contract, the house rules, the BH guide and your own data in the app).
- The conversation only lives in your phone’s memory while the app is open; **we do not keep it**. We only keep the daily usage count (to prevent abuse) and the answers you **report** with "Report this answer", so the team can review them.
- Before the first use, the app explains this. Charlotte is optional: the rest of the app works without her.

### 2.5 Community moderation (artificial intelligence)
- Before other residents can see it, every post, comment or trip is checked by **Claude (Anthropic)** to stop insults, exposed personal data and scams. Anything held goes to the team, who decides.

### 2.6 Location (optional, stays on the phone)
- Only if you turn on **"Ta carte de BH"**, and only while the app is open, the app reads your position at most every 2 hours to build your monthly recap.
- The position is **rounded** (about 110 m) before it is saved and **stays on your phone**: it is not sent to Chez Toi.
- To name the neighbourhood, the rounded position is sent to the phone’s own map service (Apple or Google).
- You can turn it off at any time in the app or in the phone’s settings.

### 2.7 Recap photos
- The photos you pick for the monthly recap are copied into the app’s storage **on your phone** and are not sent to anyone unless you share the recap yourself.

### 2.8 Notifications
- If you allow notifications, we keep the **device notification identifier** (Expo push token), the system (iOS or Android) and the app language, to send Chez Toi notices (for example: request confirmed, package arrived, building notice).

### 2.9 Usage data
- Screens opened, simple actions (for example: copying the Wi-Fi, opening Uber) and searches made in the app, linked to your account, to understand what is useful and improve the app.
- **No advertising, no sale of data, no tracking across other apps or websites.**

### 2.10 On your phone
- Access to your account is kept in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Preferences (language, app tour), the recap journal and Portuguese progress stay only on the phone.

## 4. Sharing

We never sell data. We only share what is needed with providers who process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** (the app’s database and servers);
- **Anthropic** (Claude: Charlotte and community moderation). Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models and may be kept for a limited time for safety [to be confirmed against the current agreement];
- **Expo** (sending notifications), **Apple** and **Google** (delivering notifications and the phone’s map service);
- **Resend** ("forgot password" code);
- **Other residents**: only what you post in the community or leave visible in your profile, and your first name for your roommates;
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

Some providers are outside Brazil (for example in the United States). International transfers follow article 33 of the LGPD, with contractual clauses and security safeguards [to be confirmed with the lawyer].

## 6. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) you can: confirm whether we process your data; access it; correct incomplete or wrong data; ask for anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; ask for portability; know who we share it with; withdraw consent; and complain to Brazil’s data protection authority (ANPD).

- **Correct**: on first access, with the record correction button; afterwards, by asking the team in the app or on WhatsApp.
- **Delete your account**: in the app, **Profile › Delete my account**. The account is deleted at once, with everything that only belongs to the app (profile, community posts, reviews, visitors, packages, notification devices and what the app kept on the phone). The contract, payments, expenses shared with roommates and the acceptance of the house rules stay with Chez Toi for its legal obligations. You can also ask by e-mail. Details at https://housing.cheztoibh.com.br/app/excluir-conta.
- **Other requests**: [privacidade@cheztoibh.com.br — to be confirmed]. We answer within 15 days.

## 7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, a database only reachable through our server, secure storage on the phone and team access limited to those who need it. No system is 100% secure; if an incident may put you at risk, we will tell you and the ANPD.
