# DUO Gourmet — main visual reference (owner's choice, 2026-10-16)

The owner likes this app's design and asked to use it as the inspiration for the Chez Toi app. Screenshots in `duo/`. Take the **patterns**, never the identity: Chez Toi keeps its red/white and its logo.

## What makes it work
1. The brand is on every screen (the colored top bar).
2. Photos are the content; text is short and bold.
3. Light gray canvas + white rounded cards with a very soft shadow = depth without weight.
4. Every section explains itself with a one-line subtitle.
5. Friendly, colorful category illustrations instead of generic line icons.
6. Floating pill tab bar.

## What we do NOT copy
- Burgundy and gold (DUO identity) → Chez Toi red `#E80C20` and white.
- Green "Use agora" tags (their business model).
- Uppercase titles.
