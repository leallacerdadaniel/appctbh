# Inspiration — research of 2026-10-16

Why these references: apps widely praised for design and usability, with a calm, content-first style that fits a student housing app.

| App | What we take |
|---|---|
| Airbnb (2025 redesign) | White background, content first, brand coral used sparingly (main CTA and active tab only), friendly rounded shapes, few custom icons. |
| Things 3 | Restraint: one accent color, hierarchy from type size and spacing, lists without boxes. |
| Apple Weather / Health | Large numbers and titles, one strong piece of information per block, generous margins. |
| iOS Settings (inset grouped lists) | Rows with leading icon, title, value and chevron; hairline dividers inset from the text. |
| Apple Design Awards 2026 (Tide Guide, Moonlitt) | Color only when it carries meaning; one focus per screen. |

Common principle: **every visual difference must mean something.** If two things look different, they must be different; if a decoration does not help the student find or understand something, remove it.
